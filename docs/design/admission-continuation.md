# Recover an expired shared admission without erasing its effects

Status: proposed recovery contract, following the bounded Astra review for #227.
The finite owner conversation is implemented separately. The recovery authority
below is not implemented or a receiving acceptance receipt.

The retained synthetic journey exhausted its fixed authorization after both old
senders were revoked and both old Matrix services were quiesced/preserved. Owner
reconciliation under the actual native writer confirmed that its retained root
invitation command was never dispatched: no private owner request, no exact
invitation event and no entrant acceptance event. This finding is specific to
that operation, not permission to assume every missing result had no effect.

## One linked authorization for the remaining effects

Use the existing protected configuration/journal route to approve a closed,
immutable continuation. Keep the original operation, intent, children, horizons,
native requests and results unchanged. Bind the continuation to:

- The original operation/intent digest and its latest protected effect frontier.
- The exact child lifecycle operations, association and preservation receipts.
- Owner-reconciled native RPC/event outcomes, including uncertainty or acceptance.
- The same identities, Tribe, receiving candidate, SDK and compatible fallback.
- Any separately qualified owner/coordinator helper, with exact code/context pins.
- The precisely remaining effects and one new fixed horizon for those effects.

The helper pin is distinct from the receiving generation. A new coordinator
cannot send session flags to old frozen owner code, or rewrite child settings to
adopt a helper. Select an explicitly approved compatible helper through the
linked continuation while preserving the original receiving generation and
child associations. Verify helper support from its protected public inventory;
the owner still qualifies its exact code/SDK before opening custody.

Consume the continuation consistently in social authoring, old-body replacement
starts, entrant initial startup, late all-required activation and technical
communication. Renewing only the invitation would strand the operation at the
next frozen bound. Revalidate native membership and the freshly stopped original
services before authoring; an immutable preparation receipt is not current state.

The continuation must not mark the original operation compensated to evade its
conflicting-operation guard, copy observations to an unrelated new parent, or
treat a new authorization as an exact retry of an old native request. Active
runtime/capability validity and native store bindings remain independently checked.

## Native request disposition decides the next permitted effect

An exact completed native RPC returns its authenticated historical result. A
pending RPC remains uncertain and requires exact native recovery. Any actual
acceptance requires forward recovery from that signed event. Verified absence
allows considering a newly approved effect, not silently refreshing its old frame.

If the original invitation is absent or expired without acceptance, retain its
disposition and issue a new invitation identity/exact request under the linked
authorization. Never reuse an old invitation identity with different dates.
Preserve any existing signed events and correlate their replacement explicitly.
